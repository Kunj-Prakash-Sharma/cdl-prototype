| D73 | Leads (callback requests) rules | Nov 5, 2026. A callback needs a **finished report** (the lead keeps its chat attempt id). The learner is the logged-in user, never an id from the form. A learner has **at most one NEW lead at a time**, enforced by a partial unique index: repeat taps and two tabs make one lead, the repeat gets the same lead back (`created: false`, not an error), and only the call that created it may alert the team. After the team marks a lead contacted or closed, a new request is allowed. The lead stores the **phone as a snapshot** (the number consented to), role (derived, D71), preferred time (morning, afternoon, evening), consent time and consent-text version; the **name is not copied** (read through the auth service). The learner never gets the phone back. Requests are rate-limited per learner (5 an hour); the phone-OTP login stands in for a bot check. Deleting a user deletes their leads. Consent wording is data with a version and a DRAFT flag (lawyer review, open-questions). The `minor` flag on the age options is question-set data. **Admin side:** list newest first with a status filter and counts, status change (new, contacted, closed; back to new is refused while the learner has another new one), and a CSV download (UTF-8 with BOM, Indian time, phone written as text with a digit first, cells that start with = + - @ neutralised, 2000 rows at most). Every admin function takes the session and refuses non-admins (the action does `requireAdmin` first). The admin list shows the raw phone and the live profile name. **Team email (E6-S3):** sent by a pg-boss job once per NEW lead, to one env address (`CALLBACK_ALERT_EMAIL`, #4668 open, optional until decided); the lead is saved first, so a failed enqueue or email never loses it; the job retries, throws loudly when the address is missing, marks `alerted_at` so a retry never double-sends. The email carries the phone, who to ask for, preferred time, age band and top interests, and **no name**; logs carry the lead id only. Extends D71 |
| D74 | OTP abuse: device limit and SMS spend cutoff | Nov 6, 2026 (E3-S2). Two new layers on top of D64's per-number and per-IP limits. **Device limit:** a random id in an httpOnly cookie, stored only as an HMAC; 10 codes per device per day in production (100 elsewhere). It stops one person hammering one browser, NOT a bot (a bot drops cookies): the IP limit, Turnstile and the cutoff do that work. **SMS spend cutoff:** one shared counter, `SMS_DAILY_CAP` codes per rolling 24 hours (default 5000 in production, practically off elsewhere; sized for a launch to about 8k invited subscribers); when reached, no code is sent to anyone until the window ends and the learner sees a generic "try again later" message. Counted AFTER the bot check so a bot without a token cannot use up the budget. The cutoff covers SMS only (no AI spend, D62). Both reuse `auth_otp_throttle` (keys `dev:` and `sms:total`), so no migration. New codes `device_limit`, `sms_cutoff`. **Per-IP limit raised from 10 to 60 per hour** (mobile carriers and campuses share IPs, so 10 would block real learners at launch). Extends D64 |
| D75 | Resuming an unfinished chat | Nov 12, 2026 (E4-S12). A learner who comes back to an open attempt with at least one saved answer, **after being away for `CHAT_INACTIVITY_MINUTES` (30 min, D58) or more**, sees a **choice screen: "Continue" or "Start again"**. A quick refresh mid-quiz, or an attempt with no answers, still resumes silently (otherwise every refresh would ask again). **An open attempt expires after `CHAT_RESUME_DAYS` (default 30, about 1 month) without an answer**; last activity = `last_answer_at`, or `started_at` if nothing was answered. Expiry is computed when the attempt is read (no background job, no new column): an expired attempt is marked `abandoned` and the next start creates a fresh one. "Start again" abandons the attempt the screen showed (the call names its id, so a stale tab cannot end a different one) and starts a new one (answers are kept, the attempt just never resumes). This is separate from D58: `CHAT_INACTIVITY_MINUTES` (30 min) only decides when `chat_exited` fires; the attempt stays resumable until the resume window ends. Extends D58 |
| D76 | Sweep for unalerted leads | Nov 12, 2026 (E6). A lead is saved first and the team email is queued after, so a queueing failure or an alert job that ran out of retries left `alerted_at` empty and the team never heard. A pg-boss **schedule** (new `schedule(job, cron)` on our queue interface, so modules never touch the SDK) runs `leads.alert_sweep` every 5 minutes: it re-queues the alert job for leads with `alerted_at` empty, **older than 5 minutes** (so it does not race the original job) and **younger than 3 days** (so a missing `CALLBACK_ALERT_EMAIL` is not retried forever), oldest first, 50 per run, singleton key = lead id. Safe because the alert job skips an already alerted lead (idempotent). No migration. Extends D73 |
| D77 | Admin login with email + password | Nov 12, 2026 (E3-S6). Admins can also log in at `/admin/login` with email + password; phone-OTP admins stay as a second way in (the client still has to confirm the method). **No sign-up:** accounts are made only by `npm run admin:create -- <email>`, which prints a random 24-character password once (no first-login change, no forgot-password: a reset is `--reset` by us). Sign-in goes through Better Auth's handler so the Turnstile check and a per-IP limit apply; 5 wrong passwords lock that email for 15 minutes (reuses the OTP lock table, counted for unknown emails too). One error for an unknown email and a wrong password (no account enumeration). Email-admin accounts are separate from phone accounts. Extends D64 |

| D80 | Guest accounts (visitor tracking) | Nov 16, 2026. A visitor who taps Start gets a **Better Auth anonymous user** (plugin inside `modules/auth` only), made at that tap and not on page load, behind the bot check and the D74 limits. Kept **7 days**; a pg-boss job deletes anonymous users older than that, with their unfinished attempts. When the phone is verified, auth records `linkedUserId` on the guest row and hands over the typed name (newest wins); Better Auth is told **not to delete the guest** (`disableDeleteAnonymousUser`), because the foreign key cascades and the answers would vanish. The chat then **re-points** (not copies) its rows lazily and idempotently (`claimLinkedGuests`, called at the top of the report and quiz start), so it works whether they verified at the gate or on /login. The worker claims, then deletes guests older than 7 days. `getSession()` returns null for a guest, so every existing page, action and the navbar treat a guest as logged out; only the quiz uses `getVisitor()`. `requireUser()` refuses anonymous users, so leads, buying and paid video stay closed; a separate `requireLearnerOrGuest()` is used by the chat only. Free lecture previews (D66) make **no account**: an anonymous analytics id only. Analytics session id stays the attempt id and gains a random `visitor_id`; no personal data (D18). Admin user lists and counts hide anonymous users. Extends D66, D74 |

## Architecture-doc decisions (D1–D40)
See the table in [requirements/architecture.md](requirements/architecture.md#decisions-made). The ones that shape daily work:

- **D15** modular monolith, managed Postgres, object storage, job queue.
- **D17** products, orders and entitlements live in our own database (orders ≠ entitlements).
- **D18** no raw chat text stored; journey tracked by stage, message number, input type, exit point.
- **D35/D36** fixed-question guided chat, no open text box (see D46 for the one exception, the name).
- **D40** no DRM at launch; signed expiring links plus a viewer watermark.
- **Age** asked in the chat only; no gate in Phase 1 (client's choice, lawyer review recommended).
