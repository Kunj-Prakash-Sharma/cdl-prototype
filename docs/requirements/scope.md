# SELF Phase 1 – Scope of Work

Source: "SELF Phase 1 – Scope of Work.pdf", Oct 19, 2026, Kunj Sharma. Converted to markdown for repo use. The PDF is the original; if they disagree, ask.

## What we are building
SELF is a website where students and parents take a free, friendly career chat, get a simple personal report, and then buy self-paced courses to grow their leadership and career skills. Phase 1 is the first working version, opened to invited users only.

Two parts:
- **Level 0:** a free career discovery chat with Meera, our friendly guide.
- **Level 1:** self-paced courses that the user buys once and keeps.

Everything else (live sessions, mentors, colleges, certificates) comes in later phases. "SELF" is a working name until the final name and website address are decided.

## Who will use it
- **Learner:** a student or a parent who takes the career chat, reads the report, and buys and watches courses.
- **Client team (admin):** adds courses, sets prices, and sees users, purchases and callback requests.

## The user journey
Home page → Login (phone + OTP) → Meera chat (free, about 25 quick taps, 5 to 7 minutes) → Free report (strengths, interests, save or download PDF) → Course page (first lesson free, rest locked) → Pay online (course or bundle, receipt by email) → Learn in My SELF (videos, PDFs, quiz, progress, resume).

Optional side action from the report: **Callback request** (saved in admin, team gets an email). A user can also open any course directly from the home page.

## What is included in Phase 1

### Home page
- Short introduction to SELF, a big "Start career discovery" button, the course list and an FAQ.

### Career discovery chat with Meera (free)
- Login with phone number and a one-time code (OTP).
- About 25 quick-tap questions in a friendly style, 5 to 7 minutes: a few basic details (name, age, studying or working, goal), then personality (OCEAN) and interest (RIASEC) questions.
- Answers are scored by fixed rules, so results stay consistent. The chat is in English.

### Free report
- Shows strengths, growth areas, top career interests and suggested courses.
- The user can see suggested courses and buy, request a callback (with a tick-box agreeing to be contacted), save the report in their account, and download it as a PDF.

### Self-paced courses
- 3 to 4 courses at launch, each with videos and downloadable PDFs.
- The first lesson is a free preview, and the rest is locked until the user buys.
- A short multiple-choice quiz after a lesson, marked automatically.
- A progress bar and "resume where I left".

### Buying
- One-time online payment for a single course or a bundle (for example 3 courses together at a lower price).
- A payment receipt is sent by email.
- The client team can give access manually, for example for offline payments.

### My SELF dashboard (for the learner)
- My courses and progress, my saved reports, profile settings (name, number, email), and an FAQ and help page.

### Admin panel (for the client team)
- Add and manage courses: videos, PDFs, quiz questions, price, category, description, maximum discount, and publish or hide.
- A list of users and what they bought.
- A list of callback requests, with download to Excel. The team also gets an instant email for each new request.

### Tracking
- We track where users stop in the chat and on the pages, so the client can see where people drop off. Numbers are viewed in a ready-made analytics tool.

## Not in Phase 1 (coming later)
- **Phase 2, around 6 December:** live sessions (Level 2), calendar and session reminders.
- **December to January:** college (B2B) onboarding and dashboards.
- **Later, dates to be decided:** 1:1 mentor booking and mentor marketplace, certificates, discussion room, doubt section and assignments, promo codes, a paid deeper report and the Career Anchor tool inside courses, Hindi screens, the MY SELF AI companion and open-ended chat, subscriptions, WhatsApp or SMS marketing, connection to a CRM, video piracy protection (DRM), a mobile app, editing home page text from admin, and an analytics dashboard inside admin.

## Points required from the client
1. **Prices and refunds.** Final prices, bundles and the refund policy.
2. **Sensitive messages.** If a user writes words about self-harm, Meera shows a helpline link and stops the chat. Please confirm the helpline and wording.
3. **Report wording.** The report gives career guidance and is not a medical or psychological test result. Please approve this wording.

Phase 1 includes only what is listed under "What is included". The items under "Not in Phase 1" come later. Changes after approval are handled as change requests and may move the release date.
